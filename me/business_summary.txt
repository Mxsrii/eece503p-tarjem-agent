TARJEM CERTIFIED TRANSLATION BUREAU - BUSINESS SUMMARY

Tarjem (the Arabic word for "translation") is a certified translation bureau based in Hamra, Beirut, founded in 2016. We translate the documents that people need for study, work, residency and legal matters abroad: civil records such as birth and marriage certificates, academic records such as diplomas and transcripts, and legal or commercial documents such as contracts, powers of attorney and company registrations. We work between Arabic, English and French, in any direction (Arabic-English, Arabic-French, English-French). We do not currently offer any other languages.

Our mission is to make official paperwork less stressful by being clear, fast and honest. Every certified translation is done by a sworn translator, then checked page by page by a second reviewer. Prices are published and flat, per page, so customers know the cost before they commit. Before any order starts, we run a short intake checklist (document type, language pair, destination country, number of pages) to catch common formatting problems early. We never promise that a particular embassy, university, court or ministry will accept a translation; that decision belongs to the receiving authority, and we do not give legal or immigration advice.

Prices (USD, per page of up to 250 words): civil documents 18, academic documents 22, legal and commercial documents 30. Notarization and ministry legalization coordination is 25 per document, plus government fees, which are set by the authorities and are not included. Rush delivery (within 24 hours) adds 50 percent; same-day express (order before 11:00, maximum 3 pages) adds 100 percent. The minimum order is 15. Standard turnaround is 2 business days for civil documents, 3 for academic documents and 4 for legal and commercial documents of up to 5 pages; longer documents are quoted individually by our team. Delivery is a stamped PDF by email; a hard copy can be couriered within Beirut for 5 or elsewhere in Lebanon for 8. We do not ship internationally. Free corrections are included for any translator error reported within 30 days. Source files are kept confidential, covered by NDAs signed by all translators, and deleted after 90 days.
